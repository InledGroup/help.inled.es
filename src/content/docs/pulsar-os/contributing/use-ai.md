**UPDATED**

The Linux world is divided by many fronts of opinion (although there is one predominant one). Many are against the use of AI in Linux for ideological or technical reasons and when they discover a project made by AI they discredit it and look for any error to attack the project.

Well that's another thing Pulsar OS doesn't get into. Pulsar OS is developed with the help of AI and tested in depth. The Pulsar OS community uses AI and there is nothing wrong with using it, you just don't have to go overboard.

When contributing to Pulsar OS, keep in mind:
- AI cannot replace you, do not delegate communication to agents. Don't make PR's or issues with AI, add your point. This is because it is a pain not to feel that you are talking to a human, in addition to the fact that the agent is your responsibility.
- Don't redo things or edit large numbers of files.
- Focus on things that are missing or broken, not on redoing everything.
- If you want to add a functionality, tell us first and then you can start a MVP (with AI, no problem) and send us the repo so we can finish it
- What you send has to be tested and work, and to make it more likely to be published, it is good that it is reviewed and mature.

With these simple rules, we can do things with AI.

If you have any questions, stop by our chat channels and we will help you :)  

In resume, **we dont hate AI, we encourage you to use it**
