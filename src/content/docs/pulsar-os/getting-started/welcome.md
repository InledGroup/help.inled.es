Pulsar OS is a bold bet that has never been attempted in Linux. Most Linux distributions limit themselves to minimally customizing the look of a desktop environment or shipping a system with a pre-configured setup. But that alone won't let Linux compete with the operating systems dominating the personal desktop market. Linux has been built on its own terms, from the perspective of the professional developers who create it, without truly considering the everyday user or what the market actually needs. Distro after distro keeps appearing with the same formula: a new icon pack, a fresh desktop environment, a handful of tweaks, an installer, and that's it. That won't cut it. If we want Linux to reach the level of macOS (I won't even mention Windows, because Windows 11 is a pain in the neck), we need to learn what makes those systems special. Why do people choose macOS? Why do some leave it?

Pulsar OS is the system that has come to change everything.

Unlike most Linux distributions, Pulsar OS is built around a friendly community with members at all levels of experience, always open to welcoming newcomers, no matter how much time they can dedicate. Personally, I am tired of the Linux community, where decisions depend on a single person or a small group of insiders. Likewise, the Pulsar OS community is made up of people from all walks of life, and frankly, nobody cares about your political views because politics have no place here.

You are welcome. We need your help, and we will be glad to have you contribute in any way you can.

You can read our documentation to learn more about Pulsar OS and our stance on AI. In short: we don't prohibit it, we love it, but we don't go overboard.
